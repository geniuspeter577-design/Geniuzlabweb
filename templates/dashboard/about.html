Our Story

How It All Started

GeniuzLab began with a simple observation — countless talented young creatives had the skill to build real careers in design, video and technology, but nowhere near enough guidance, tools or opportunity to turn that talent into income. What started as one person's passion for graphic design, video editing and artificial intelligence grew into a mission to close that gap.

Step by step, GeniuzLab evolved from a single creative service into a full ecosystem — Geniuz Academy for learning, GeniuzKonnect for hiring and getting hired, Geniuz Graphics and GENIUZinMOTION as in-house creative studios, and GeniuzSubs for everyday digital services. Every addition was built around the same journey: learn a skill, create real work, connect with opportunity, and grow.

Today, GeniuzLab's vision is bigger than any single product — it's to become the go-to home for creative professionals everywhere, a place where talent meets technology and turns into a sustainable, thriving career.

Meet The Founder

Otsaje Genius Peter

Founder & Chief Executive Officer, GeniuzLab

Otsaje Genius Peter is the Founder and CEO of GeniuzLab, a creative technology ecosystem built to help a new generation of digital professionals learn, create, and earn. Blending a background in graphic design, video production, and artificial intelligence, he set out to build a single platform where creative skill, modern technology, and real economic opportunity meet.

Under his leadership, GeniuzLab has grown into an ecosystem spanning creative education, an AI-powered marketplace for hiring and getting hired, in-house design and motion studios, and everyday digital services — all designed around one goal: empowering young creatives to turn their talent into a sustainable career.

He is driven by a simple mission — to make world-class creative and technology skills accessible to anyone willing to learn, and to build the tools that help them turn that skill into income, opportunity, and impact.
